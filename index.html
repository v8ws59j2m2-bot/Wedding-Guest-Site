We'd love you to join us for our wedding in Bali.

We're planning a 10-day villa stay from 5th to 15th April 2028, staying together at a beautiful private villa estate in Canggu. The wedding day is still to be confirmed, with the rest of the trip for relaxing, celebrating and enjoying Bali together.

If you'd like to arrive when the villa stay begins on 5th April, you'll likely need to fly on 4th April because of the travel time and time difference.

We know this is a big trip, so we wanted to share the estimated costs early to help you decide whether it feels affordable. There's absolutely no pressure — we completely understand that a destination wedding is a big commitment.

Dates at a Glance

Trip Dates5th–15th Apr 2028
LocationCanggu, Bali
Stay10 nights
Wedding DayTo be confirmed

We've tried hard to choose dates that work for as many people as possible. The 2028 Easter holidays aren't as aligned across schools as usual, and with 9 UK schools plus Italian schools to factor in, it hasn't been possible to find a perfect window for everyone. Easter was always our preferred time to celebrate, and it also helps avoid the higher prices of the summer holidays. We really hope these dates work for the majority, but completely understand if they don't work for everyone.

What's Included

  • 10 nights at a private villa estate in Canggu
  • Breakfast included every day
  • Premium private catering for lunch and dinner for 9 days
  • Separate catering included for the wedding day
  • Private chefs and butlers on-site
  • A small selection of drinks with meals, plus some daytime snacks
  • – Other drinks and snacks can be purchased from the venue or local shops
  • Use of the villa estate, pools and shared spaces

You're very welcome to eat out at local restaurants or beach bars whenever you like. However, the villa catering will already be included and paid for as part of the package.

Villa Highlights

  • Exclusive use of the private estate with 12 individual wooden villas
  • 12 private swimming pools throughout the property
  • Lush tropical gardens with beautiful rice field views
  • Fully staffed from 7am to 11pm
